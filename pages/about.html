About ROJGARDWAAR.IN

Free exam eligibility information and practical tools for preparing application documents.

What is ROJGARDWAAR.IN?

ROJGARDWAAR.IN is an independent website that currently focuses on free, browser-based application tools and exam eligibility information. It helps visitors prepare common document files and check general eligibility details in one place.

Tools currently available

Photo & Signature Resizer

Prepare photo and signature images by adjusting dimensions and file size for application forms. Always compare the output with the exact requirements in the relevant official notification or application portal.

Document Scanner

Use images to create a clearer scanned-document style output, adjust the crop and image appearance, and download the resulting file. The tool does not certify or officially verify documents.

Image to PDF / PDF to Image

Combine supported images into a PDF or convert PDF pages to JPG images in your browser. Check the downloaded output before submitting it.

Exam Eligibility Calculator

Use the available eligibility tool to review exam information based on the details and rules in its database. Results are estimates and must be checked against the latest official notification.

What is temporarily unavailable?

Science notes and practice pages, generic mock-test entry points, Railway test-series listings and the student dashboard are temporarily hidden while we review content quality and the user experience. These sections will only be presented again after the relevant checks are complete.

Accuracy and official information

Recruitment dates, age limits, qualifications, document dimensions and file-size limits can differ between application portals and notifications. Always follow the requirements published by the relevant official authority.

Important notice

ROJGARDWAAR.IN is an independent website. It is not the official website of any government department, Railway Recruitment Board, SSC, NCERT or other examination authority, and it does not claim affiliation or endorsement by them.
