About ROJGARDWAAR.IN

Empowering government job aspirants with accurate eligibility information.

Our Mission

ROJGARDWAAR.IN was created with a simple mission: to help millions of Indian youth preparing for government jobs understand their exam eligibility quickly and accurately. We believe that every aspirant deserves clear, reliable information about which examinations they can apply for, without having to manually cross-check dozens of official notifications.

What We Offer

  • Universal Eligibility Calculator: Check your eligibility across SSC, UPSC, RRB, Banking, Defence, Teaching, State PSC and other major recruitment examinations in one place.
  • Accurate Age Calculation: Our calculator uses the exact cutoff dates specified in official notifications, not just today's date.
  • Category-Aware Analysis: Proper application of OBC, SC, ST, EWS and PwD age relaxation as per each exam's official rules.
  • Qualification Matching: Smart qualification hierarchy that understands higher qualifications satisfy lower requirements.
  • Direct Official Links: Every result includes links to the official notification and application portal.

Our Approach

We prioritize accuracy and transparency above everything else. We do not invent eligibility rules or create fake official links. Every piece of data in our calculator is sourced from official government notifications and is structured to be easily updated when rules change.

Why Trust Us?

  • We never claim to cover "all exams in India" unless our database actually does.
  • We clearly display a disclaimer on every result, reminding users to verify with official notifications.
  • We link only to official government and recruitment authority websites.
  • We do not collect or store your personal data.
  • Our tool is completely free to use.

Continuous Improvement

We are constantly working to expand our exam database, improve calculation accuracy, and add new features that genuinely help aspirants. If you have suggestions, found an error, or want us to add a specific exam, please reach out through our contact page.

Not an Official Source

ROJGARDWAAR.IN is an independent informational platform. We are not affiliated with any government organization, recruitment board, or examination authority. Always verify all eligibility details from the official notification before applying for any examination.
