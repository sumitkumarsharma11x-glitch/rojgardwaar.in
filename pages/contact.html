Contact Us

Have a question, suggestion, or want to report an issue? We'd love to hear from you.

Get in Touch

We welcome feedback, exam database update requests, bug reports, and general inquiries. Please note that we are an informational platform and cannot help with individual application issues or official recruitment queries.

Common Reasons to Contact Us

  • Request to add a new examination to our database
  • Report incorrect age limits or relaxation data
  • Report broken official links
  • Suggest new features or improvements
  • Report technical issues with the calculator
  • Partnership or collaboration inquiries

Email Us

For general inquiries and feedback:

contact@rojgardwaar.in

For technical issues or bug reports:

support@rojgardwaar.in

Response Time

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 48-72 hours during business days. Please allow additional time during weekends and public holidays.

What We Cannot Help With

We cannot assist with individual application form filling, payment issues, admit card downloads, or any queries related to the official recruitment process. For such matters, please contact the respective recruiting organization directly through their official channels.
