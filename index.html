How This Eligibility Calculator Works

Our eligibility calculator is designed to help Indian government job aspirants quickly determine which competitive examinations they may be qualified to apply for. The tool works by comparing your personal details against the official eligibility criteria of various recruitment examinations.

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Enter Your Date of Birth: The calculator uses your exact date of birth to compute your age as of the specific cutoff date defined for each examination.
  2. Select Your Category: Choose your reservation category (General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, or PwD). Each exam has specific age relaxation rules for different categories.
  3. Select Your Qualification: Indicate your highest educational qualification. The tool checks if your qualification meets or exceeds the requirement for each exam.
  4. Instant Analysis: The calculator processes all exams in our database and instantly shows your eligibility status for each one.

What Makes This Tool Accurate

  • Each exam uses its specific cutoff date — not today's date. Different exams use different reference dates for age calculation.
  • Category relaxation is applied per exam, not universally. Some exams may not offer relaxation for certain categories.
  • Qualification hierarchy is respected — higher qualifications are accepted for exams requiring lower qualifications.
  • The database is structured to be easily updated when official notifications change age limits or eligibility criteria.

How Age Relaxation Works

Age relaxation in Indian government recruitment is not uniform across all examinations. Each recruiting organization defines its own relaxation policy in the official notification. Understanding how relaxation works is crucial for accurate eligibility assessment.

Common Relaxation Patterns

  • OBC (Other Backward Classes): Typically 3 years of age relaxation beyond the general category upper age limit.
  • SC/ST (Scheduled Caste/Tribe): Typically 5 years of age relaxation beyond the general category upper age limit.
  • PwD (Persons with Disabilities): Usually 10 years of age relaxation, though this varies significantly by exam.
  • EWS (Economically Weaker Section): Generally no age relaxation, but eligible for reservation in seats.

Important Notes

  • Relaxation rules can change with each new recruitment notification.
  • Some exams (like CDS, NDA) do not offer any category-based age relaxation.
  • Certain state-level exams may have different relaxation policies than central government exams.
  • The calculator uses the most commonly observed relaxation values, but you must verify against the specific notification.

Key Insight

Always check the official notification for the exact relaxation applicable to your category for a specific examination. Our calculator provides an estimate based on standard patterns, but official notifications may have special provisions or exceptions.

What This Tool Does Not Cover

  • Physical fitness requirements for defence and paramilitary examinations.
  • Nationality and domicile requirements for state-level examinations.
  • Specific subject requirements or stream restrictions (e.g., science stream for technical posts).
  • Experience requirements for certain specialized posts.
  • Number of attempts limitations (e.g., UPSC CSE attempt limits).
  • Marital status requirements for certain defence examinations.

We strongly recommend that you read the complete official notification of any examination before filling out the application form. This calculator is a starting point for your research, not the final authority on your eligibility.
