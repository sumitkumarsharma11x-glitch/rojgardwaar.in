Where are the positions?

The published report describes five contractual Sericulture Assistant positions across the Central Sericultural Research and Training Institute (CSB-CSRTI), Berhampore, and associated units. It identifies one position at the Regional Extension Centre in Shillong, Meghalaya, and one at the CSB Regional Extension Centre in Mangaldoi, Assam. The other reported positions are outside the North-East, so applicants should check the location and unit in the official advertisement.

Who may be eligible?

The eligibility requirements depend on the relevant sericulture sector and unit. The report mentions qualifications from areas such as sericulture, agricultural sciences, forestry, biological sciences or biotechnology, with additional requirements such as a postgraduate diploma or relevant experience for some categories. Experience in silkworm rearing and grainage is described as desirable for the pre-cocoon sector.

This is only a broad summary, not an eligibility decision. Do not assume that a degree alone is sufficient. Read the official advertisement for the exact qualification, required experience, age limit, age cut-off date and any category-specific conditions.

How to apply

  1. Open the official Meghalaya recruitment listing and open its attached notice.
  2. Confirm the post, location, sector, qualifications, age limit and deadline from the full advertisement.
  3. If the notice confirms online applications through the reported portal, use csb-hiring.in and follow only the official instructions.
  4. Prepare self-attested qualification and experience documents only as requested by the notice. Keep a copy of the submitted form and acknowledgement.
  5. Check the Central Silk Board website for corrigenda or updates before the deadline.

Selection process and expenses

Secondary coverage reports selection through a personal interview and says eligible candidates will be informed of interview details. It also reports that travel, daily allowance and lodging/boarding expenses will not be provided. Treat these points as provisional until confirmed in the official advertisement.
